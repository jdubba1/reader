Keep the companion running

Reader needs a small Python companion to store and search your archive. Captures stay on this computer. There is no Reader account or cloud sync.

  1. Install Python 3.10 or newer from python.org.
  2. Download the Reader source using Code → Download ZIP, then unzip it.
  3. Open Start Reader.command on macOS, or Start Reader.bat on Windows. On Linux, run python3 scripts/companion.py in that folder. Keep the terminal open while using Reader.
  4. Click Check connection & sync above. Enable capture, then reload your X tabs and scroll.

After restarting your computer, open the launcher again. If disconnected, check the companion window for an error. Move the whole Reader folder together so your archive stays with it. See the README for startup shortcuts and troubleshooting.

What capture saves

Once enabled, Reader saves posts loaded by X, including posts you may not read, their authors, text, quoted text, links, media URLs, available metrics, and reading, like, and bookmark signals. It does not download media. Read means at least 1.8 seconds at half visibility. Capture can miss posts when X changes.

Paused capture saves no new posts or signals. Already queued items continue syncing. Up to 5,000 posts and 5,000 signals, within a 6 MiB queue limit, wait in Chrome storage; oldest items are dropped when full. Privacy policy

Export or delete

Export includes the companion's X archive as JSON. Sync first to include queued captures. Instagram imports are separate and are not included here.

This clears the X archive and this extension's queue and capture counters. Capture stays paused. It cannot delete downloaded exports, backups, or the separate Instagram database.
